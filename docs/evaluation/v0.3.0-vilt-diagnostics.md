## Decision / 结论

The fixed answer vocabulary is not the dominant overall ceiling: 97.79% of questions have a representable human answer and the vocabulary-constrained oracle score is 0.9337. The larger observed gap is between that ceiling and the model's 0.6945 full-split project score. This points to answer selection and reasoning errors as the higher-priority investigation, while names, time expressions, and some numeric answers remain targeted vocabulary gaps.

固定答案词表不是整体表现的主要上限：97.79% 的问题至少有一个可表示答案，词表约束下的理论软分为 0.9337。更大的观测差距来自理论上界与模型全验证集项目软分 0.6945 之间。这说明应优先排查答案选择与推理错误；专名、时间表达和部分数字答案仍是明确的局部词表缺口。

For the next controlled run, retain the Top-3000 vocabulary and address the measured generalization gap by training only the final six ViLT layers. Use the diagnostics to judge whether a later, separately controlled vocabulary expansion is warranted. No human error review was performed for this report.

下一轮受控实验应保留 Top-3000 词表，通过仅训练 ViLT 最后 6 层来处理已观察到的泛化差距。之后是否扩大词表，应由另一项独立受控实验决定。本报告未进行人工错误复核。
